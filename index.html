FAQ

Common questions

Who can donate blood?

Healthy adults who meet the age, weight and hemoglobin guidelines above and have no disqualifying condition. The donor registration page runs a quick check, and a doctor confirms.

How often can I donate?

Whole blood donors should wait at least three months between donations.

What if I am not eligible right now?

Some reasons are temporary, such as an infection or a recent tattoo. Your registration shows how long to wait so you can donate later.

What should I do in a life-threatening emergency?

Call 108 or go to the nearest hospital immediately, then post a request here so donors can be arranged.

Before you register
